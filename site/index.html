What I do

Payments, income and the reconciliations behind them

At the Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames I handle all of the council's housing accommodation payments, covering temporary accommodation and private sector leasing. That is between £3.5 million and £4.5 million a month. I run the rent checks for the Private Sector Leasing scheme and complete the monthly and quarterly reconciliations behind the payments.

Before Kingston I worked in Sanctuary's Central Income Team on rent and service charge accounts and arrears. Earlier, in Pune, I prepared business valuations and financial models and supervised a team of 7 to 9 analysts. I hold an MSc in Financial and Business Management (Merit) from Kingston University.

01

Payments & invoicing

Accommodation payments from invoice to payment. Invoices checked against bookings, occupancy and agreed rates, and coded to the right GL and cost centre. Suppliers go through due diligence before they are paid.

02

Income recovery & rents

PSL rent checks against lease terms. Rent and service charge accounts, arrears found early and affordable repayment plans. Universal Credit and Housing Benefit income. Overpayments and unused funds recovered.

03

Reconciliations & controls

Monthly and quarterly reconciliations across NEC, Business World and the tracker. Vendor accounts and purchase ledger, month-end close and fraud investigations. Working papers kept ready for internal and external audit.

04

Reporting, systems & automation

NEC system champion for the Housing team. Monitoring reports and briefings for managers who are not finance specialists. An invoice-checking tool in development in Google Sheets and Apps Script.

AI in finance operations

Checking invoices before they are paid

Most payment checking is matching: invoice against booking, billed rate against agreed rate, nights billed against nights occupied. Fixed rules catch the known errors. A simple anomaly score then highlights invoices that look unusual compared with the rest, so a person reviews them before any money leaves.

This is the idea behind the invoice-verification tool I am building in Google Sheets and Apps Script. The demo uses made-up data.
